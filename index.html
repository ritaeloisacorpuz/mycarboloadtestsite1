Brain Food

Unlock Your Potential

Unlike carbohydrates or sugar, protein is a complex molecule. There are many types of protein and each one has a specific role to play in your body – most protein is necessary for tissue repair while some are for normal locomotor activities but the most important are the ones necessary for brain function. Consuming the right protein gives your brain the right capability for clear cognition and sharp intellect. BrainFood Dishes are specially prepared to heighten your cognition, prep you up for a mentally taxing day or simply keep you alert in class.

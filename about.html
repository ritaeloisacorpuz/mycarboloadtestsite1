About Us

This is who we are

We are parents, educators and business women alarmed at the declining English literacy and dropping proficiency in Math and Science. So, we banded together to help busy parents provide their kids with the skills they need to become who they dream to be in life — an Engineer, a Doctor, a Lawyer or maybe the first Filipino Nobel Prize winner? Or maybe the next best-selling author? The possibilities are unlimited.

With this mission in mind, we formed a group of dedicated educators who derive deep satisfaction in helping your children achieve academic excellence — our Power Tutors.

Our Power Tutors will design and tailor your academic program to meet your child's specific needs. We will assess your child's learning style and chart his learning milestones appropriate to his skills and capabilities.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing top-notch tutorial services to students from grade school to college. With a strong focus on academic excellence, we offer personalized tutorial to meet the unique learning needs of each student. Our team of experienced educators is passionate about helping your child succeed.

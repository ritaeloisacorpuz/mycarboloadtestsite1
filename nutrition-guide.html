CarboLoad is more than just a food brand, it is actually a term for a practice athletes do, specially those involved in marathons. CarboLoad, which is a shortened term for “carbohydrate-load” refers to maximizing the storage of glycogen in an athlete’s muscles and liver. Since glycogen is the body’s most readily accessible and efficient source of energy during prolonged, intense exercise, athletes intentionally consume high amounts of carbohydrates a day or night before a competition effectively packing their "energy tanks" to the brim so that the following day when they are in the middle of their activity, the onset of fatigue and the dreaded mid-race physical collapse are delayed, sustaining peak endurance.
